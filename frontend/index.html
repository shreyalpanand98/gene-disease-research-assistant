Helix brings the Gene–Disease Research Assistant into one workspace. Its initial research focus is PKD1, PKD2, and ADPKD, with live human gene lookups and PubMed searches for other questions.

  1. Plan & retrieveA planner interprets your question, looks up gene records, and retrieves up to three PubMed articles.
  2. Synthesize & reviewThe assistant summarizes the evidence, checks citations and relevance, and assesses scientific claims when required.
  3. Explore the evidenceRead the sources, inspect individual claim assessments, and export permitted summaries and references.
A research companion, with limits.

Passing automated checks does not establish scientific correctness. Withheld answers stay hidden; there is no human-review queue. Ask for a simpler explanation to revisit the previous answer and its sources.

Conversations stay in this server’s memory, expire after 24 hours of inactivity, and disappear when it restarts. Export anything you want to keep. Do not enter private patient information.
